## 1. Intentional Legal & Operational Constraints
1. **Live Payment Partner Agreement Pending:** Production merchant acquiring contracts are not yet activated. The platform operates safely in high-fidelity Sandbox mode.
2. **Game Currency Gating:** Game currency transactions are restricted by default in jurisdictions requiring formal legal clarification under gaming and AML statutes.
3. **Game Account Trading Strictly Banned:** In compliance with Article 3.4, credential exchanges and account trading are disabled to prevent account recovery disputes and publisher ban waves.
