## 1. App Store & Google Play Policy Alignment
- **Google Play:** Policy guidelines regarding digital content transactions require clear distinction between in-app purchases (IAP) for consumption within the app versus third-party physical or external platform game digital items.
- **Apple App Store:** Guideline 3.1.5 (Cryptocurrencies & Digital Goods): Third-party marketplace transactions must not circumvent Apple payment rails for iOS native content. For cross-platform game digital goods, mobile app primarily serves browsing, delivery proof review, and order status tracking.
- Native build pipelines: Target Android App Bundle (AAB) and iOS Archive (IPA).
