# 29. PRIVACY POLICY DESIGN & DATA RETENTION

**Document ID:** BN-DOC-029  
**Ref:** Master PO Execution Order BN-GAMEEX-MASTER-001 (Article 21 & 31)  

---

## 1. Compliance with GDPR, CCPA & PIPA
- **Data Minimization:** Only minimal account details (email, country, display name) collected.
- **Redaction of Delivery Evidence:** Chat and evidence records containing player credentials or real names are automatically flagged for PII masking.
- **Right to Erasure:** Inactive user records can be anonymized upon request; however, financial transaction records and audit logs are retained for statutory accounting periods (5-7 years) per commercial law requirements.
